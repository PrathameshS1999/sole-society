Privacy

Your basket is kept in your own browser. If you place an order, create an account or send a message, those details (your name, email, delivery address, orders, saved addresses and wishlist) are stored in the shop's database, hosted by Supabase. Passwords are handled by Supabase's sign-in service and are never stored by this site.

Terms

This is a demo store. No real orders are taken, nothing is shipped and no card is charged.

Cookies

No tracking. This site sets no cookies; your basket and sign-in session are kept in your browser's local storage.
